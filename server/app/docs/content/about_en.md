# About Us

Cosmic is a mobile game focused on reaction, attention, and consistent progress. We build a product that works reliably across devices, avoids unnecessary permissions, and stays usable even with unstable internet connectivity.

## Our Mission

- Deliver a fair and straightforward gameplay experience without hidden mechanics.
- Keep user profiles and progress synchronized so data remains available across devices.
- Ensure predictable performance: fast startup, stable updates, and clear rules.

## How the App Works

- An account is used for sign-in and data synchronization.
- Profile data and game metrics (for example, record, rating, balance) are stored and updated via the app's server-side components.
- Some information may be temporarily stored on the device (cache) to improve speed; when connectivity is available, data is verified and refreshed against the server.

## Security and Responsibility

We use modern data protection practices, including storing passwords on the server only in hashed form. Security also depends on the user: do not share access with third parties and use a strong password.

The app may include links to third-party services or partner platforms. Users make their own decisions when interacting with such resources; the Developer is not responsible for the content, terms, or availability of third-party services.
