# Game Rules

## 1. Goal
Your main goal is to **reach a Balance of 100 or higher**.

## 2. Game Session
A game session starts when you press **Start** and ends when the game finishes or when you leave the game screen.  
During a session you earn points and try to improve your result.

## 3. Points and Attempts
- **Points** are awarded for successful actions in the game.
- **Attempts (lives)** give you several chances to improve your result within a single session.
- **Reward** can provide extra options to continue the game and/or get additional attempts.

## 4. Record
**Record** is your best points result. It is saved after sessions and shown in your profile.

## 5. Rating and “Clean Play”
**Rating** reflects your skill and the quality of your play.

Rating depends not only on the number of points, but also on how **“cleanly”** the result was achieved:
- results without extra continues/boosts are valued higher;
- results achieved using rewards may have a smaller impact on rating.

In short: **the more fair and consistent your performance, the higher your rating**.

## 6. Balance
**Balance** shows your progress toward the goal (**100+**).

Balance is formed from two parts:
- accrual based on time in the game (banner part);
- accrual based on viewing rewards (reward part).

At the same time:
- **Record and quality of play → affect Rating**
- **Rating → affects Balance**

## 7. Fair Play
The game uses mechanisms to prevent unfair play.  
If suspicious performance is detected, the session results may be **rejected** and your progress may be **reset**.

## 8. Profile
Your profile shows:
- **Record**
- **Rating**
- **Balance**

Data may update after a session ends and may be synchronized with the server when a connection is available.

## 9. Settings
In the app settings you can:
- **change the interface language**;
- **adjust controls/layout of elements** (if available in the current version);
- change other interface and usability options.

## 10. User Responsibility
You are responsible for:
- keeping your account access details safe;
- following fair play rules;
- your actions on third-party services/partner platforms if the app provides links to them.
